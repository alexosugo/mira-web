# Sellogram Wordmark

## Approved source

The approved creative direction is `Sellogram`: uppercase `S`, lowercase `ellogram`, rendered as one cohesive word in heavy rounded geometry.

The original approved attachment has SHA-256:

`36b161f6841a52f048372713f05bed37a53290fc4b800042d4dc27e01617b845`

`source/source-uppercase.png` is a lossless 1-bit normalized crop of that approved attachment for deterministic geometry extraction. Its SHA-256 is:

`5bc6c4e4411aaa1bba346f5b4e3c22c98f1965d55fc45eff4af5672c8cc57bec`

The normalized source contains no redesign decisions. It only removes surrounding whitespace and anti-aliasing so the approved geometry can be segmented reproducibly.

## Gate 1 result

Three refinements of the same approved concept were evaluated:

- `faithful` — closest to the supplied drawing; geometry cleanup only.
- `disciplined` — stronger optical consistency, spacing, rhythm, and balance.
- `characterful` — preserves the concept while pushing its strongest successful traits slightly further.

**Disciplined was selected and approved as the production wordmark.**

The authoritative master is:

`master/sellogram.svg`

The master is a byte-for-byte copy of `candidates/disciplined/sellogram-disciplined.svg`. Faithful and Characterful remain historical review candidates only.

Raster previews are not authoritative. Production rendering must originate from the SVG master.

## Identity rule

Sellogram is always one word. Do not create an `SG` monogram or any other initial-based secondary identity.
