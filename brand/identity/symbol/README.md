# Sellogram compact identity

The approved compact identity is **S / Pure**: the capital `S` taken directly from the production Sellogram wordmark.

The full Sellogram wordmark remains the primary identity wherever space allows. The compact `S` is for favicon, app-icon, avatar, and other small-format uses where the full wordmark cannot fit.

## Production master

- `master/sellogram-symbol.svg`
- selected candidate: `candidates/s-pure/sellogram-s-pure.svg`

The master and selected candidate are byte-for-byte identical. The `S` outline is the exact capital `S` geometry from `brand/identity/wordmark/master/sellogram.svg`, optically centered by the square SVG viewBox without redrawing the glyph.

## Rules

- Never use `SG`.
- Never create an `SG` monogram.
- Do not split Sellogram into initials.
- The compact `S` does not rename or abbreviate the brand in copy. It is a small-format identity asset only.
- Avoid Instagram camera/ring visual territory.
- Use the full Sellogram wordmark instead of the compact mark whenever the available space supports it.
- Vector SVG masters are authoritative. Raster files are generated outputs only.

## Historical Gate 2 explorations

The earlier `loop`, standalone `g` fragment, and `ogr` crop explorations are rejected historical candidates. They are retained only as design history and must not be used as active Sellogram identity assets.
